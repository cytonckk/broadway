Why a map, and not an essay

I could have written all of this as a paper, but I think the whole point of Broadway's history is that it's a story about location — about an industry physically dragging itself up an island, block by block, chasing its audience. That's something you can read about, or it's something you can watch happen. I'd rather you watch it happen.

Every stop on this timeline was chosen because something structural changed there: not just "a famous show opened," but the district moved, or the business model changed, or the city pushed back. That's the standard I used to keep this from becoming a list of every theater that ever existed.
